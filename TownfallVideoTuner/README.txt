Townfall Video Tuner 1.0.1
Your own videos on the CRTV in SILENT HILL: Townfall.

INSTALL
1. Install UE4SS for SILENT HILL Townfall (https://www.nexusmods.com/silenthilltownfall/mods/4).
2. Copy this TownfallVideoTuner folder into Townfall\Binaries\Win64\ue4ss\Mods.
3. Put your videos directly in TownfallVideoTuner\Videos.
4. Start the game, load your save, raise the CRTV and tune to a magenta mark.
   Videos\stations-auto.ini lists the frequency each video got.

YOUR VIDEOS
- Fewer and smaller videos work best. The CRTV's screen shows 640x480, so a 480p
  or 720p video looks the same. Larger videos can make the game stutter. One, two
  or three videos are plenty.
- MP4 and MKV only. Picture: H.264 or H.265. Sound: AAC.
- Dolby sound (AC-3, E-AC-3: "DD5.1", "DDP5.1", "EAC3"), DTS and TrueHD play
  without sound. Convert their audio to AAC first, or find a different version.
- Restart the game after adding or removing videos.
- The mod can affect the game's performance: playing a video takes the game extra
  work. If the game stutters, use fewer and smaller videos.

SETTINGS
stations.ini is optional: where the stations go, stations at frequencies of your
own, loop and volume. Its comments say how.

UPDATING
Close the game and copy the new folder over this one. If you changed
stations.ini, keep your copy. Your videos stay.

WORKS WITH Townfall Companion 2.0.2 or newer
(https://www.nexusmods.com/silenthilltownfall/mods/125): the videos also play
on the phone's CRTV, and their marks show on the phone's dial.

PROBLEMS
Read tf-video.log in this folder (tf-video.previous.log is the game before).
